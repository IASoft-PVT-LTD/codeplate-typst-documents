The sample business, clients, addresses, numbers, bank details and the example time-tracker export (`data/imports/`) are made up. Addresses use real street names for realism; the businesses don't exist, and the `.example` domains can't receive email.

When you add assets, list each one in `assets.json` (path, size, where it's used, licence, credit) and here, with its author, licence and a link to its source. Only use images you own or made, CC0 images, and fonts under the OFL (or a similar licence that allows embedding), with their licence file next to them.
